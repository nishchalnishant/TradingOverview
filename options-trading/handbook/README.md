## Core Principles

- **Options are leveraged instruments.** A small move in the underlying can produce large percentage gains or losses. Size accordingly.
- **Theta decays every day.** Long options bleed value; short options collect it. Know which side you are on.
- **Implied volatility is the most important input.** Buying overpriced IV or selling underpriced IV destroys edge regardless of directional accuracy.
- **Defined risk structures exist for a reason.** Spreads cap both profit and loss — they reduce cost and eliminate margin blow-up risk.
- **Manage positions before expiration.** Most professional options traders close positions early rather than holding to expiry.
