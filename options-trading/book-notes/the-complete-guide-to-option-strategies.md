# The Complete Guide to Option Strategies

By Michael Mullaney

Michael Mullaney’s guide focuses on the practical application of options strategies for the modern trader. It emphasizes systematic approaches and clear rules for entry, exit, and adjustment.

## Core Philosophical Tenets
- **Options as a Business:** Trading options should be treated as a business with defined processes and risk management.
- **Probabilities over Predictions:** Focus on the statistical probability of success rather than trying to "guess" the market's next move.

## Key Strategy Breakdowns

### 1. The Power of Credit Spreads
Mullaney is a strong advocate for being a "net seller" of options.
- **Bull Put Spreads:** Profiting from a stock staying above a certain level.
- **Bear Call Spreads:** Profiting from a stock staying below a certain level.
- **The Edge:** How being the "house" (the seller) gives you a statistical advantage over time.

### 2. Neutral Income Strategies
- **Iron Condors:** Deep dive into how to select the right "deltas" for your short strikes to balance risk and reward.
- **Adjustment Rules:** When and how to "roll" a position if the market moves against you—this is a critical part of the book.

### 3. Directional Spreads
- **Debit Spreads:** Using vertical spreads to lower the cost of a directional bet compared to buying naked options.
- **Comparing Debit vs. Credit:** When one is superior to the other based on Implied Volatility.

### 4. Advanced Synthetic Positions
- **Synthetic Long/Short Stock:** Using options to mimic the movement of stock with less capital.
- **Collar Strategies:** Protecting a long stock position using a combination of a long put and a short call.

## Risk Management and Trading Psychology
- **Position Sizing:** Detailed rules on how much capital to allocate to each trade type.
- **The "Greeks" in Practice:** How to monitor your portfolio's total Delta, Vega, and Theta to ensure you aren't over-leveraged in any one direction.
- **Staying Mechanical:** The importance of sticking to your plan and not letting emotions (fear/greed) dictate your trades.

## Conclusion
*The Complete Guide to Option Strategies* is a practical, no-nonsense manual for traders who want a clear, rules-based approach to the markets. It is particularly valuable for its focus on position management and adjustments—the parts of trading that happen *after* the trade is placed.
