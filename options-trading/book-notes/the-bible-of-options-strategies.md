# The Bible of Options Strategies

By Guy Cohen

This book serves as a comprehensive reference guide to over 60 different options strategies. It is organized by market outlook, making it an invaluable tool for traders to quickly find the right trade for any situation.

## How the Book is Structured
Each strategy is presented with a consistent format:
- **Market Outlook:** (Bullish, Bearish, Neutral, Volatile)
- **Risk/Reward Profile:** Clear visual diagram.
- **The Setup:** Which options to buy and sell.
- **Maximum Profit/Loss:** How to calculate them.
- **Breakeven Points:** The formulas.
- **Context:** When to use it and what to watch out for.

## Key Strategy Categories

### 1. Basic Strategies
- Covered Calls, Protective Puts, Long Calls, Long Puts.

### 2. Vertical Spreads
- Bull Call Spreads, Bear Put Spreads, Bull Put Spreads (Credit), Bear Call Spreads (Credit).

### 3. Volatility Strategies (Non-Directional)
- **Straddles and Strangles:** Profit from a big move in either direction.
- **Guts:** A straddle using in-the-money options.

### 4. Sideways Strategies (Income)
- **Iron Condors and Iron Butterflies:** The staple for non-directional income traders.
- **Ratio Spreads:** Selling more options than you buy (advanced and risky).

### 5. Advanced Combination Spreads
- **Butterflies and Condors:** Refined ways to profit from a specific price target.
- **Backspreads:** Using a combination of options to profit from a massive move with limited risk on one side.

### 6. Time-Based Strategies
- **Calendar Spreads (Horizontal Spreads):** Profiting from different rates of time decay between short-term and long-term options.
- **Diagonal Spreads:** Combining time and price movements.

## Strategic Decision-Making
Cohen emphasizes:
- **Implied Volatility (IV):** Whether to be a "net buyer" (low IV) or a "net seller" (high IV).
- **Time to Expiration (DTE):** How to choose the right expiration based on the strategy's "Theta" profile.

## Conclusion
True to its name, *The Bible of Options Strategies* is a reference manual that belongs on every trader's desk. It provides the technical blueprint for almost any trade you might want to execute, ensuring you understand the mechanics before putting capital at risk.
