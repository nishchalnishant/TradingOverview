# Section 02 — Financial Ratios

Financial ratios distill the raw numbers from financial statements into standardized, comparable metrics. They are tools for asking better questions, not definitive answers — no ratio is meaningful in isolation. Always compare ratios to: (1) the company's own history, (2) industry peers, and (3) the current macro context.

## Universal Principles for Ratio Analysis

**1. No ratio is meaningful alone**
A P/E of 15x is cheap for a high-growth tech company and expensive for a declining retailer. Always ask: cheap or expensive *relative to what?*

**2. Trends matter more than levels**
An ROE of 18% is fine; an ROE declining from 28% to 18% over five years is a warning sign. Direction tells you as much as the current level.

**3. Quality behind the number**
A high ROE driven by excessive leverage is fragile. A high P/S in a money-losing business only makes sense if there is a credible path to profitability. Always ask *why* a ratio is what it is.

**4. Accounting choices distort ratios**
FIFO vs. LIFO inventory affects margins. Operating vs. finance leases affect leverage ratios. SBC affects earnings-based multiples. Understand what the company's accounting choices are doing to the ratio.

**5. Adjust for non-recurring items**
Use normalized (adjusted) earnings for earnings-based ratios. One-time items can make a P/E look artificially low (one-time gain) or high (one-time charge).

## The DuPont Framework: Decomposing Profitability

The DuPont analysis decomposes ROE into its drivers, making it the most powerful single analytical framework for profitability:

**ROE = Net Margin × Asset Turnover × Equity Multiplier**

Where:
- Net Margin = Net Income / Revenue (profitability)
- Asset Turnover = Revenue / Total Assets (efficiency)
- Equity Multiplier = Total Assets / Equity (leverage)

Two companies with identical ROE can have completely different risk profiles:
- A company with 20% margins and no debt (Apple)
- A company with 4% margins and high leverage (a grocery chain)

The DuPont decomposition is covered in detail in [profitability-ratios.md](profitability-ratios.md).
