# Section 05 — Macro and Sector Analysis

Macro analysis is the top layer of the top-down investment framework. Even a high-quality business with a strong moat can face significant headwinds from the macro environment — rising interest rates compress valuations, recession hammers cyclical revenues, and inflation erodes real returns.

Understanding macro forces doesn't replace company analysis — it complements it. The best outcome is identifying high-quality companies at attractive valuations in a macro environment that supports their sector.

---

## The Top-Down Framework

```
Macroeconomic Environment
  (GDP growth, interest rates, inflation, monetary policy)
          ↓
Sector Selection
  (which sectors benefit/suffer in this macro regime)
          ↓
Industry Analysis
  (competitive structure, growth stage)
          ↓
Company Selection
  (moat, management, valuation)
```

Macro sets the context; sector rotation determines where to focus; company analysis identifies the best individual opportunities within the favored sectors.

## Key Principle

Macro analysis does not require macro forecasting. Few investors (including professional economists) have a persistent edge in forecasting GDP, inflation, or Fed policy. What macro analysis does is help you:

1. **Understand the current regime** — where in the cycle are we now?
2. **Assess risk** — what macro scenarios would hurt your positions?
3. **Identify tailwinds** — which sectors/themes have structural macro support?
4. **Avoid headwinds** — which sectors face structural macro pressure right now?

Don't try to predict the macro. Understand it and position accordingly.
